  <h1>Two notebooks, one bag.</h1>
  <div class="post-content">

One notebook is for what happened: the bus that never came, the woman who fed me anyway, the way the light sits differently over a Portuguese roofline than it does anywhere else. That notebook becomes the **travel** pieces on this site — stories, mostly, told slowly, on the theory that a place deserves more than a listicle.

The other notebook is for what I couldn't stop arguing with myself about afterward: borders, belonging, the ideologies we pack without noticing we've packed them. That notebook becomes the **essays** — opinionated, occasionally wrong, written to be pushed back on rather than nodded along to.

*Compass & Ink* is where the two notebooks meet. The compass points somewhere. The ink tries to say why it mattered.

---

I'm Sid — I write both, usually in the wrong order, usually with someone's coffee going cold beside the page. If you want to argue back, [email is open](mailto:{{ site.email }}), and so is the [RSS feed]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }}) if you'd rather just read.
