categories: [travel]
location: "Jaipur, India"
excerpt: "I talked him down from 200 rupees to 150. He probably wanted 150 the whole time."
tags: [india, jaipur, haggling]

The opening number was 200 rupees for a ride that should have cost 100. I knew this because the hotel had told me, unprompted, the way hotels here always do, like handing you a small piece of armor on your way out the door.

We settled at 150 after the kind of negotiation that isn't really about the fifty rupees at all — it's a script, performed by both sides with visible enjoyment, ending in a handshake and a "you are good customer" that I'm fairly sure gets delivered to everyone regardless of how the haggling actually went.

Halfway to Hawa Mahal he pointed out a building I hadn't asked about, then another, then insisted on stopping so I could photograph the palace from an angle I wouldn't have found myself. None of this was in the fare. He didn't mention it was worth extra. I gave him a tip at the end that probably erased whatever he'd saved by holding firm at 150, and I think we both knew that was always how this was going to end.
