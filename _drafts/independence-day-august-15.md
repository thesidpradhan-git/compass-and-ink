> ```
> ऐ वतन ऐ वतन आबाद रहे तू
> ```

Child of screens and scrolling light,\
freedom is not just a fight\
against a flag, a foreign hand —\
it lives in how you hold your land.

You call it free, this breathing air,\
but is your mind unbound, aware?\
Do you scroll past hate, or pause to see\
the chains of noise, of vanity?

Independence now is not the gun,\
nor marching feet beneath the sun \
it's choosing truth when lies are cheap,\
it's staying woke, not just to weep,

but to *act* — pick up a broom, a pen,\
teach a child, stand for other men.\
Freedom's not a day gone by,\
it's daily choice, it's how you try.
